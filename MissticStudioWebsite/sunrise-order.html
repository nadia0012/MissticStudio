About the Game

Fly towards your dream of building your own farm, just like in your favorite games. Since you don't have the chance to inherit a farm from your grandfather, you'll take matters into your own hands. Use your knowledge to overcome any obstacles . that arise and succeed in living off your harvests. You'll need to use a delivery service known as Animazon to better equip yourself. You will be able to purchase everything you need to succeed in your contracts and become a successful small farmer.

Key Features

  • Animazon, a delivery service to order your tools and seeds.
  • Complete orders from your customers in the form of contracts to unlock new constructions.
  • Clear new plots of land on the island to expand your farm.
  • A complete skill tree that even allows you to extend the days.
  • A touch of humor that arises when the character compares real life to their experiences in video games.

Pro's: Cute art style, pleasant music and enough game play to keep you engaged. Don't try to rush the game. Just relax, take you're time and learn the mechanic's. Very enjoyable break from the real world. Con's: Day timer run's a little to quick for the distance of walking travel as opening the fast travel portal's are later game. Final: Well worth the asking price.

RPGe**

This game is everything it says it is. A farming game with some foraging, fishing and mining to supplement your goal of being a successful farmer. It’s fun and addictive to play when you just want to shut your brain off after a hard day. Early on is a bit slow like most of these games are and there doesn’t seem to be a huge story or any npc interaction. But if you want a farming game to grind in this could be it.
