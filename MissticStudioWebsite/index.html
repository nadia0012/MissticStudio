About Us

Sarah Doyon Co-Founder & Artist
Gabriel Rolland Co-Founder & Developer

Founded in February 2022 by Sarah Doyon and Gabriel Rolland, Misstic Studio is an independent Canadian video game development studio.

Equipped with a graphic tablet and a keyboard/mouse, we join forces to complete our quests entitled Sunrises Order and Craftiny. Our games are designed to be charming, colorful and fun, to give players a good time.
